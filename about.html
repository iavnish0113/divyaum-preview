Who we are

Bringing Sanatan to your home

Divyaum is founded on a simple belief: every person deserves access to divine grace, wherever in the world they live.

In 2025, our founders set out to build a home for Sanatan wisdom and legacy — one that respects the sanctity of ancient rituals while using modern technology to bring them closer. Today we work with pandits, temples and a growing community of devotees to bring authentic poojas, guidance and sacred essentials to your doorstep.

Why we exist

5,000 years of wisdom, in every ritual

In every bead, every flame, every mantra chanted and every ahuti offered, we bring you the wisdom and legacy of Sanatan Dharma.

Sanatan Dharma was never meant to be a Sunday ritual or a festive obligation. It was meant to be the rhythm of your life — woven into how you wake, how you work, how you love and how you breathe. Divyaum exists to bring that rhythm back.
