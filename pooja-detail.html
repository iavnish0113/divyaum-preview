Questions

Before you book

Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp — 11am to 6pm, every day.

Do I need to be present for the pooja?

No. Certified pandits perform the pooja at the temple with your name and gotra in the sankalp. You receive a recorded video of the pooja afterwards.

Can I add family members?

Yes. The price is ₹2,100 per person, and each person’s name and gotra is taken in the sankalp.

When will I receive the prasad?

Prasad is dispatched 2–4 business days after the pooja is completed, and delivered by courier — in India and to select countries abroad.

What if I need to cancel?

100% refund if you cancel 48 hours or more before the pooja, 50% between 24 and 48 hours, and 25% within 24 hours. If Divyaum cancels, you receive a full refund.
